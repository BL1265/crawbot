About CLAWEX

CLAWEX combines Artificial Intelligence, robotics and automation to create a smart waste sorting system. A camera detects waste, AI classifies the item, and the robot sorts it into the correct recycling bin.

Features

📷 Live Camera

Detects waste objects in real time.

🧠 AI Recognition

Identifies waste categories using AI.

🔵 Bluetooth

Connects AI software with Arduino.

🤖 Robotic Claw

Moves waste into the correct bin.
